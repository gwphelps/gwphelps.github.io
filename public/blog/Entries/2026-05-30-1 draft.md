
## On the Lolicon "Jokes" in Monogatari

One of my favorite anime is The Monogatari Series. This is something that, depending on the audience, I may not mention to other people. The reason is that Monogatari makes a LOT of jokes about being attracted to young girls. In the grand scheme of the series it's not that much of the total screen time really, but it's so blatant and over-the-top that it's impossible to ignore. Despite this, I do genuinely love the show. This is something I find myself needing to reconcile within myself in a way, and this dichotomy is something I want to explore a little. 

### 1. It's Clearly A Joke
This is my first argument defending the lolicon stuff that happens in Monogatari: It's a joke. The scenes where things like this happen are almost always being played for laughs. There are series like Mushoku Tensei where the perversion is much more sinister and explicit, and not just being played as a joke. Monogatari is not like this. It's a show that can be serious when it wants to be, but the lolicon stuff is almost never played as serious in that way.
