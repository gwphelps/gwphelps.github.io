## Drinking Yuri ep 5
I watched episode 5 of Botan Kamiina Fully Blossoms when Drunk. This show is such a delight to watch every week. This week especially it looked absolutely gorgeous, probably the best it's looked so far, which is saying something. Additionally I really like how the show lets awkward and/or intimate moments sit for a few seconds without people stammering or trying to fill the silence. It really sets the tone and allows implication to set in when someone says something that is a little charged.
