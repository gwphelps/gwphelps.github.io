
## Secrets of the Silent Witch: It was Good.

I finished Secrets of the Silent Witch. All in all it's pretty light fare, I would argue too light but we'll get there. Monica is a pretty fun protagonist, it's great to see her come out of her shell a little bit by the end of the series. The magical fights look great, especially when Monica is popping off. The humor was hit or miss for me but at it's best it's endearing and fun, at it's worst a little cringey. 

I guess my main complaint is that there is just not enough to chew on thematically for my tastes. Monica has a compelling backstory where her dad got convicted of heresy due to his studies, and then between being potentially abused by her foster parents and getting bullied at school it's no wonder she turned out the way she did. But throughout the story I never really got the impression that her backstory actually mattered that much. They could have explored something like how trauma causes you to push people away, even if they are trying to help you and care about you. But honestly I kind of got the impression that the backstory was mostly just there because Monica needed some backstory. 

I think what I'm trying to say is that they watered down Monica's personality to fit closer to a mold of a generally socially anxious person, so that people like that would find her relatable. Rather than making her more like an actual traumatized individual. 

Monica also cries a lot. A lot of the time it's essentially a comedy bit, or I guess more like it's supposed to be cute and endearing. But it makes it hard for me to care during the actual emotional scenes when she cries because I've been conditioned to think cry = funny. This is a problem I have with a ton of anime so it's nothing especially new, but the hard transitions between light comedy and serious moments can be a little jarring.

The music was great, pretty minimal in general but then they would turn on the jets during the magical scenes to raise those up. The OP and ED are great, I mentioned in a previous post that I started watching this show exclusively because of the ED, and just hearing through the grapevine that it's a good show.

Overall, cute show. Despite my misgivings it was enjoyable to watch and I would probably watch season 2 if it ever comes out. I think it will, my understanding is that the light novel is really popular.
