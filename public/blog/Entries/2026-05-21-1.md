## Bocchi the OP Witch

I started watching Secrets of the Silent Witch. The reason I started the series was because I randomly came across the ED on YouTube, and liked the song. I liked it not just as an anime song but as something I would listen to outside of the context of anime too. I thought the artist looked familiar so I looked them up on my Spotify and found that not only had I listened to them before, but I had a couple of their songs saved from back in 2021. Hitsujibungaku. So, not for the first time, I started watching an Anime based solely on liking the ED. Turns out the OP was also made by the same band and it's arguably even better so that was a treat. 

The anime itself I'm pretty lukewarm on so far. It's a fantasy, not an isekai, which I like. The main hook of the show is that Monica is a super OP witch, essentially mages in this world. But she also has crippling social anxiety, which through her own ingenuity resulted in her learning to cast magic without incantations, apparently a big deal in this universe. 

I've seen people compare her to Bocchi from Bocchi the Rock, another character with pretty severe social anxiety. While on a surface level I'm inclined to agree, there is one major differentiator between them that I think is important to call out. Bocchi has a mental disorder, and it feel a little mean to say but she is the way she is in spite of her surroundings. She has a loving family, and the other people at school don't ostracize her per say, they just don't actively look to connect with her either. 

Monica is different. From what I've seen in the first two episodes, her situation is actually trauma. We don't have all the details yet but it seems like a combination of bullying in her younger years at school, and issues at home, resulted in the person she is today. A meek, deferential shut in who is also one of the strongest mages in the region. I'm interested to see how they expand on this, because exploring trauma and the way it haunts you could make for very engaging storytelling, compared to the social anxiety being just a character trait that the protagonist slowly gets over due to the help of the people around them. 

The animation is ok, they use CGI quite a bit for some of the stuff. The art itself looks very nice, and they clearly put a lot of effort into both the art and animation during the magic sequences because those in particular look very, very good. 

One of the major characters is played by Junichi Suwabe, which is not surprising since he's in everything, but it's always a treat.

Anyway, let's just see how it plays out. I'm for sure going to watch at least a few more episodes. In all likelihood I will probably finish it, I think it's only 12 episodes since it just came out a year ago.
