
## Hibike! Euphonium Season 2: I kinda think it's cry-porn but it's still really good.

Hibike! Euphonium is a show that I keep finding myself liking almost in spite of myself. Whenever I think about the show I don't really find it particularly interesting, but in the moment while watching I am absolutely engrossed. Season 2 was much more serious on average than season 1 I thought. Especially during the early-mid part of the season it really felt like Kumiko was having one tough conversation after another. 

I think the place I want to hone in on is Kumiko as a character. I think it's fair to say that season 1 was about Kumiko finding passion for band and the euphonium again. In season 2 we learn that part of the reason she is like this is because of her sister Mamiko, who quit band in order to focus on college. Her sister's dismissive attitude toward band rubbed off on Kumiko and she was struggling to see the point. But through the hard work leading to success in season 1, Kumiko re-found some of that passion for the instrument, practicing so hard she got a nosebleed. So by the end of season 1 Kumiko is a much more active participant in band, and someone who wants them all to be successful in the competitions.
